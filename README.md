## Willem B. Bruin

I’m a postdoctoral researcher in **precision psychiatry**: the effort to tailor psychiatric diagnosis and treatment to the individual patient, rather than to the average of many. I hold positions at [Amsterdam UMC](https://pure.amsterdamumc.nl/en/persons/willem-bruin/) and
[Leiden University](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/staffmembers/willem-bruin).  I work across obsessive-compulsive, anxiety and mood disorders, using brain scans from thousands of people, machine learning, and studies that follow the same individuals over many years, to understand individual differences rather than the “average” patient. Most of it happens through international consortia:
[ENIGMA-OCD](https://enigma.ini.usc.edu/ongoing/enigma-ocd-working-group/),
[ENIGMA-ANXIETY](https://enigma.ini.usc.edu/ongoing/enigma-anxiety/) and
[GEMRIC](https://mmiv.no/gemric/).

### What you'll find here

Preprocessing and analysis pipelines for MRI data, and code behind the
consortium papers listed below. A model that only works on the data it was built
from hasn't told us much, so I try to keep the methods inspectable.
